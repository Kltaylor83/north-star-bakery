Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery built on a love for fresh bread, delicious pastries, and bringing people together. Our goal is to make every customer feel welcome while providing quality baked goods.

We believe great baked goods begin with quality ingredients. We focus on fresh ingredients, careful preparation, and supporting local suppliers whenever possible.
